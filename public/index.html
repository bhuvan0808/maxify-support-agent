About this assistant
  • It is an AI, not a person. It can be wrong; every action comes with a reference you can quote.
  • It can answer questions about your orders, products and policies, and carry out eligible refunds, exchanges and cancellations.
  • A person decides fault claims, larger refunds and anything unusual. You get a ticket reference and a reply time.
  • It cannot change who you are signed in as, set refund amounts, or override store policy.
  • Your data: your messages and order details are processed to handle your request, including by our AI model provider. Card and ID numbers are removed automatically; please do not send them.
  • Talk to a person: just ask for one, or email support.
